**Download**
- **AionComics_1.3.1_x64-setup.exe**: the installer. Installs AionComics and lets you open .cbz and .cbr files with it.
- **AionComics_1.3.1_x64_portable.exe**: the app on its own, no install needed. Just run it.

On 1.3.0, AionComics offers this update by itself: look for "1.3.1 is ready" next to search. Installing over an older version keeps your library, reading progress and settings.

### Look
- **Tidier text.** Titles across the app use one set of sizes, so things at the same level match. Section titles in Your reading and Library check now look like the ones on Home, and search groups its results under small, clear labels.
- **Easier reading.** Descriptions and creator bios wrap at a comfortable width instead of running across the whole window, and titles no longer leave a single word alone on their last line.

### Movement
- **Rating stamps.** The rating on the card at the end of a book thumps down like a rubber stamp each time you rate.
- **Sheets set down like a panel.** What's new, Find on Comic Vine and Edit details drop into place, their caption box landing a beat after.
- **Covers arrive in order** when you open a library page, and the numbers in Your reading count up.
- **Smoother all round.** Big library pages open without the brief freeze they had, and scroll more evenly. Book details close as smoothly as they open, and the page and the side panel now slide together.
- **Follows Windows.** If animations are turned off in Windows and you haven't chosen otherwise in Settings › Look, AionComics keeps still too.

### Fixes
- **Clearer storage setting.** Settings › Storage now reads "Keep books ready to open for", with a note saying what happens after that long.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
