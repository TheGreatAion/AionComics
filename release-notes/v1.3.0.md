**Download**
- **AionComics_1.3.0_x64-setup.exe**: the installer. Installs AionComics and lets you open .cbz and .cbr files with it.
- **AionComics_1.3.0_x64_portable.exe**: the app on its own, no install needed. Just run it.

Installing over 1.2 keeps your library, reading progress and settings. From this version on, AionComics updates itself.

### Updates
- **AionComics keeps itself up to date.** New versions download in the background. When one is ready, "1.3 is ready" shows next to search and the settings gear gets a dot. Restart from there whenever you like, or later.
- **See what's new before you update.** Each update comes with a list of what changed, and the first start after updating shows it once. Settings › Updates has your version, a Check now button, and a switch to turn automatic downloads off.

### Ratings
- **Rate what you read.** The card at the end of a book has five stars stamped across its cover, or press 1 to 5 while reading. Pressing the same number again takes the rating away.
- **Series ratings.** A series page shows the average of the books you rated, and you can rate the series itself. A book's stars show under its cover and in its details.
- **Sort by your best rated.** A star among the sorts on each library page puts your favorites first, and another button shows only series you rated 4 stars and up.
- **From your shelves.** Home suggests series you own and haven't started, by the writers and artists behind books you rated highly, and says why. Press + to put one on Want to read, or × to stop suggesting it.
- **Favorite creators** in Your reading: the writers and artists you rate highest, once you've rated three of their books.

### Read on another device
- **Your library on your phone or tablet.** Turn on Settings › Other devices and open the address it shows in the browser on your phone, tablet or another computer. Browse, read, and your place is kept in step with the PC. A 6-digit code keeps it to your own devices.

### Comic Vine
- **A better finder.** Each match shows its cover, its first and last issue, when it came out and what it's about. Editions from other countries are set apart and tagged with their language, and the right series comes up first more often.
- **Want to add details?** A series without details says so next to the Comic Vine button when Comic Vine has it. Without a Comic Vine key, it takes your key right there.
- **Character and creator pages** have a portrait, a short bio and where they first appeared.
- **Covers and first appearances** in a book's details: its variant covers (click one to see it large) and the characters who first appeared in it.

### Guided View
- **Better panels.** More pages are cut into the right panels, in the right order.
- **Cut and merge panels** in the panel editor, for the pages it still gets wrong.
- **Starts at once.** Panels are worked out ahead of time while you read.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
