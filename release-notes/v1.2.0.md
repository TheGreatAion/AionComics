**Download**
- **AionComics_1.2.0_x64-setup.exe**: the installer. Installs AionComics and lets you open .cbz and .cbr files with it.
- **AionComics_1.2.0_x64_portable.exe**: the app on its own, no install needed. Just run it.

Installing over 1.1 keeps your library, reading progress and settings.

### Want to read
- **A list of what you mean to read next.** Put a series on it with the bookmark button on its page, or a single book with "Want to read this book" from its right-click menu. The list has its own page, first under Library in the side panel, with a + button to find and add more. Series on the list wear a ribbon on library pages, and something leaves the list once you start reading it.

### Reading
- **Chapters in manga.** When a volume's pages mark where its chapters start (most digital manga do), the bottom bar shows the chapter you're in and how many pages are left in it. Press ] or [ to jump between chapters; the page strip marks where each one starts, and the ⋯ menu lists them all.
- **Shift pairs by one page.** In double page view, a book with one page too many or too few near the front splits every two-page picture across two screens. Shift+D moves the pairs over by one page so those pictures are whole again. It's remembered for each book.

### Guided View
- **Panels cut apart by black art are whole again.** Black hair, armor or shadows touching a dark gutter no longer split one panel into two stops.

### Your library
- **Library check.** Settings › Library › Library check goes through every book and lists the ones that wouldn't open: files that are missing, damaged or empty, and covers that won't load. It changes and deletes nothing, and waits while you read.
- **New issues.** For series you've read in the past year or put on Want to read, and that are matched to Comic Vine, the series page tells you when newer issues are out, for example "#5 and #6 are out". Checked every few days in the background; needs your Comic Vine key.
- **A plain gray reading bar** under each cover on library pages.

### Look
- **New icons** for Settings (a gear) and your reading stats (a rising line).

### Fixes
- **Some comics that wouldn't open now do.** ZIP comics whose page names contain unusual characters, like a non-breaking space, open normally.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
