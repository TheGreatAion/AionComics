**Download**
- **AionComics_1.3.5_x64-setup.exe**: the installer.
- **AionComics_1.3.5_x64_portable.exe**: the app on its own, no install needed.

On 1.3.0 or later, AionComics offers this update by itself.

### Updates
- **Update from a copy that isn't installed.** If you open AionComics from an exe you saved somewhere, and it's also installed on this PC, "Restart and update" now updates the installed copy and opens it, instead of sending you to the download page.
